# Audio credits

## Hold music

The five `moh-*.mp3` tracks are the opsound set Asterisk ships (`asterisk-moh-opsound-g722`), the
hold music Zamfono bundles, converted to MP3 by `scripts/moh/import.mjs`. They are distributed
under the [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)
licence through explicit permission from their authors.

| Track          | Artist                  | File                                 | Source                                |
| -------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| Cold Day       | Paul Shuler (Macroform) | `moh-macroform-cold_day.mp3`         | http://macroform.bandcamp.com/        |
| Robot Dity     | Paul Shuler (Macroform) | `moh-macroform-robot_dity.mp3`       | http://macroform.bandcamp.com/        |
| The Simplicity | Paul Shuler (Macroform) | `moh-macroform-the_simplicity.mp3`   | http://macroform.bandcamp.com/        |
| Morning Coffee | Manolo Camp             | `moh-manolo_camp-morning_coffee.mp3` | http://ccmixter.org/people/ManoloCamp |
| System         | Reno Project            | `moh-reno_project-system.mp3`        | http://www.jamendo.com/en/album/23661 |

## Speech

Voicemails, call recordings, greetings and announcements are synthetic speech generated for the
demo by `scripts/tts/generate.mjs`; the callers and the company are fictional.
